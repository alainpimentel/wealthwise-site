Privacy policy

WealthwiseFi — last updated October 2026

WealthwiseFi is a personal finance dashboard. This policy describes how information from a user’s linked accounts is handled.

What information is accessed

When a user links an account through Plaid, the app can read account names and balances, investment holdings and securities, and recent transactions. It cannot move money, place trades or change anything at the institution. Bank passwords are entered only in Plaid’s own window; the app never sees or stores them.

Where it is stored

  • On the user’s own device, in a local database. Access tokens that Plaid issues are encrypted there.
  • For backup, an encrypted copy can be saved to the user’s own cloud storage.

Who it is shared with

No one. The information is not sold, rented, used for advertising or shared with third parties. It passes only between the user’s device, Plaid (to retrieve the data) and the user’s own cloud storage described above.

Keeping and deleting it

A user can disconnect an institution at any time, which stops further data being retrieved, and can delete the stored data from their own device and cloud storage whenever they choose.

Plaid

Plaid handles the connection to financial institutions under its own terms. See Plaid’s End User Privacy Policy.
