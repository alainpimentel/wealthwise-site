WealthwiseFi

A personal finance dashboard.

What it is

WealthwiseFi brings a person’s accounts together in one place: net worth, investments, spending and retirement progress, with no retyping of balances.

How it uses Plaid

Users connect their own bank and investment accounts through Plaid so the dashboard can show current balances, holdings and transactions. The connection is read-only: the app cannot move money or make trades.

Your data

Information from linked accounts is stored in an encrypted store on the user’s own device and is not shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising. See the privacy policy for the details.
